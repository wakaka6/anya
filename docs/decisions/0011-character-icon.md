# 0011: The icon uses the character Anya

Accepted, 2026-10-30.

## Context

Anya is named after the character Anya Forger from *Spy × Family*, whose rights belong to Tatsuya Endo, Shueisha and the anime's producers. Original icons that only hinted at the character did not read as her. 0006 lets work from elsewhere in only when its authors agreed; the character's owners have not.

## Decision

The app icon is built from a picture of the character, with Anya's own additions (thought bubble, glow, signal arcs, star eyes, backgrounds), in a light and a dark variant. The project accepts that the owners may ask for it to be removed.

## Consequences

- The icon is the one thing in the repository outside 0006; it does not open the door to other third-party work.
- On a takedown request, or before choosing a licence or publishing a release, the icon is replaced with original artwork, and the name may have to follow.
- The menu bar template stays original.
